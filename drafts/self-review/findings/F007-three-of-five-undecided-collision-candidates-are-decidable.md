# F007 — Three of the collision audit's five undecided candidates are decidable, and two look like refutations
phase: normalization-collision audit (follow-on)   step: n/a   date: 2026-09-03T00:24Z

what happened: `evidence/normalization-collisions/audit.json` confirmed seven collisions and left five candidates as `HYPOTHESIS`, correctly refusing to count a reasoned guess as a finding. Reading the ws-core source settles or nearly settles three of the five, and two of those three look like REFUTATIONS — which matters, because a refuted candidate raises the ceiling on what the differential can see rather than lowering it, and the audit is worth as much for what it clears as for what it finds.

**CAND-UTF8 — refuted, and provably, not by sample.** The candidate asks whether two different inbound octet sequences can decode to the same `text` event, since the event records the decoded String and `utf8_bytes = len(String)` rather than the received octets. The audit says this depends on whether ws_core replaces malformed UTF-8 with U+FFFD or rejects the frame. It rejects: `Charsetfunctions::string_utf8` is `String::from_utf8(bytes).map_err(|_| Utf8DecodeError)` (`rust/ws-core/src/message.rs:187-189`), and there is no `from_utf8_lossy`, no `U+FFFD` and no replacement anywhere under `rust/ws-core/src`. UTF-8 is injective on valid input — overlong forms and surrogates are invalid and rejected — so distinct accepted octet sequences yield distinct Strings, and invalid ones yield no `text` event at all. **The candidate set is empty.** This is a proof about a total function rather than a sample, which is the strongest form this catalogue can carry.

**CAND-WIREBYTES — evidence points to refuted; needs the measurement.** The candidate asks whether a non-minimal frame length encoding (a short payload sent under the 126 extended form) is representable. Two facts bear on it. First, ws-core ACCEPTS non-minimal extended lengths deliberately: `rust/ws-core/src/framing.rs:37-39` records that the Codex codec's "non-canonical 16/64-bit length rejection is STRIPPED (Java accepts non-minimal extended lengths; derive.go:400-420 reads whatever the wire says)" — so a pair CAN be constructed, where an RFC-strict codec would have made the candidate empty. Second, `frames[].wire_bytes` is the consumed span, not a computed minimum: `frame.wire_bytes = span.size` (`rust/ws-core/src/connection.rs:662`). A non-minimal header consumes two more octets, so the value should differ and the distinction should be REPRESENTED. Strong, but it is a reading of two sites rather than a run, and the audit's standard is decision by construction.

**CAND-CHUNKING — reasoned represented; needs the measurement.** The audit already reasons that `input_chunk` events carry a per-step byte count, so a 1+1 split differs from a 2 split. It listed the candidate anyway on the grounds that a reasoned negative is not a measured one, which is the right instinct.

**CAND-TRANSPORT and CAND-CROSSARRAY are correctly undecidable in that package** and should stay HYPOTHESIS there. Transport lifecycle needs a real peer socket (ws-testee's loopback), and cross-array ordering cannot be provoked because the harness generates all three arrays from one pass. Both are registered elsewhere — CAND-TRANSPORT as DIV-02 in `internal/ac5class`, CAND-CROSSARRAY as the US-013 AC5 case.

what it cost: nothing yet — the audit did not overclaim, and this is follow-on rather than correction.

where the deciding moment was: the audit set its own bar at "decided by construction, not argument" and then honoured it by leaving five candidates open rather than reasoning them closed. That discipline is why this note can be additive instead of a retraction. The temptation it resisted is the one worth naming: three of the five were reachable with a little more work, and a weaker record would have reasoned them shut and reported twelve findings.

bin: not a defect — a completion. Two candidates should move to REFUTED once measured, one is provably empty today, and two should stay HYPOTHESIS with their reasons. A refuted candidate is a real result: it says the observation DOES carry a distinction, which narrows what the 73-of-74 and 26-of-49 ceilings can be blamed on.
